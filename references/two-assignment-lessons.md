# Lessons from two course assignments

These are historical examples of **different task shapes**, not templates or default requirements.

| Historical assignment | What the official brief required | Reusable writing lesson |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI in Innovation and Entrepreneurship | Three applied questions about an opportunity, product/business model, and implementation/economics/risks; separate suggested word ranges and scored criteria. The worked case was an anonymized education-sales CRM copilot. | Map each question and required deliverable to evidence. Show defensible market sizing, pricing assumptions, compliance and staged validation where the rubric asks for them; distinguish measured business baselines from projected ROI. |
| Advanced Project Management in AI | One real problem, two or three relevant course concepts, one risk examined in depth, trade-offs and response in one essay. The worked case was full-chain evaluation for AI-assisted short drama. | Let the brief determine the essay structure. Separate factual fidelity from video quality, proposed evaluation volume from observed data, and overseas market context from evidence of any firm's workflow. Put human labels, cross-team ownership and release gates in the management design. |

In the second workflow, an earlier English source draft used one course code, while a later PDF used a different code in its filename and front matter. The assignment PDF itself did not state a code, while a user-provided LMS naming example used the later code. The record therefore establishes a **cross-version inconsistency**, not that either code was definitively wrong. Confirm the current LMS rule, then align source, exported PDF and filename after each rename or rebuild. A body count appeared compliant, while adding front matter could exceed the upper bound. Check the stated word-count scope and both counts when it is unclear.

Both historical PDFs asked for English work within a similar total word range, but neither prescribed a universal citation style, abstract, minimum bibliography size or filename. Do not transfer those details to another course. The earlier tasks had no verified independent-review record; independent review is a recommended acceptance step here, not a claimed past result.
